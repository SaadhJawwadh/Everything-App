
### 🌟 What's New
- **Scan Text to Note (Offline OCR)**: Extract editable text directly from photos or embedded note images using `/scan` or the image menu—100% on-device.
- **Proactive Budget Alerts, Bill Reminders & Weekly Digest**: Get notified at 80% and 100% of monthly category budgets, 48 hours before recurring bills are due, and with an optional Sunday Weekly Spend Digest.
- **Group "Simplify Debts" & Batch Story Slides**: Calculate the fewest transfers to settle group split bills and share the plan in one tap, plus Share All / Save All for multi-slide Story Cards.
- **Encrypted Auto-Backups, Per-Module Locks & Screenshot Shield**: Automated backups are now encrypted with a recoverable key, Finances and Health tabs can be locked individually, and Android screenshot protection can be toggled in Security Settings.

### 🚀 Improvements
- **Full-Body Note Search & Period Notes**: Search unlocked notes across their entire body (including completed checklists), add optional notes to period logs, and jump straight to Health when tapping period alerts.
- **Cleaner Finance Analytics & Broader Device Compatibility**: Excluded account transfers from spending breakdowns, scoped monthly budgets to the current month, and marked Wi-Fi hardware as optional so tablets and Chromebooks install smoothly.

### 🐛 Fixes
- **Biometrics & P2P Authentication**: Wired the "Use Biometrics" preference with safe PIN fallback, upgraded device-to-device sync with integrity verification, and restricted Story Card AI Highlights to supported devices.
